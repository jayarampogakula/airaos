🏠 Dashboard Overview

Your operational mission control center providing system telemetry briefings and instant support access.

Why to use this section?
To capture a high-level summary of your business's daily health and verify system allocations (chats, voice minutes) without clicking through individual log lists.
When to use this section?
Every morning when logging in to review AI-generated summaries of the previous night's activity, check active visitor counts, or debug integration scripts with the support bot.
Usecase Example
Clinic Front-Desk Daily Sync (Smile Dentals)
Every morning at 08:45 AM, the lead receptionist logs into GatiDesk. The AI Daily Briefing card highlights: "Arjun Mehta (WhatsApp Lead) engaged with Receptionist Sarah. Intent: high. Keywords: 'pricing', 'dental implants'. Recommended action: Outbound follow-up via voice carrier." The receptionist immediately checks the live briefing table, reviews the chat logs, and places a direct call to secure a high-value consultation slot.

How to use (Step-by-Step Guide)

  • Step 1: Check Live Briefings: Read the AI Daily Briefing card immediately to identify high-intent leads (like Rahul Mehta) flagged for instant follow-up calls.
  • Step 2: Monitor Resource Quotas: Scroll to the "Resource Limits" card in the right column to monitor chat and voice calling limits before launching marketing campaigns.
  • Step 3: Consult the Platform Guide: Use the built-in search widget or type "How to integrate Twilio?" to receive real-time configuration steps from the chatbot.
💡 Pro-Tip: The AI Briefing is generated automatically by analyzing conversation sentiment, intent score peaks, and calendar triggers from the past 12 hours. Review it first thing to avoid missing active buyer signals.

When to use this section?

Ad Spend Optimization (Elite Realty Group)
The Marketing Director reviews monthly campaign spend. Facebook Ads generated ₹9,40,000 in revenue from 234 leads (ROI: 6.2x). Google Ads generated ₹4,10,000 from 87 leads (ROI: 3.8x). Why to use this section?
To build custom marketing sequences, automated email responders, and RAG classification steps without writing custom code.
When to use this section?

Usecase Example
Lead Nurturing Automation Flow
The administrator builds a flow:
Trigger: Website Form Submitted.
Action 1: Send welcome email immediately.
Action 2: Wait 2 Days.
AI Classifier: Classify reply. If the lead is classified as "Interested", routing triggers "Assign to Sales Team". If classified as "Not Interested", it routes to the "Deactivate Lead" sequence.

Usecase Example
Connecting Multi-Channel CRM Support (Smile Dentals)
To manage all channels inside one unified board, the administrator connects:
1. **WhatsApp Cloud**: Enters Graph API token and Phone Number ID. Configures verify webhook.
2. **Gmail Support**: Clicks **Connect Gmail** to authorize OAuth2 access, syncing customer mails.
3. **Twilio Voice**: Enters Twilio credentials and maps inbound voice numbers.
Inbound contacts from all platforms automatically feed into the central Contacts dashboard.

1. WhatsApp Cloud API Integration

Direct connectivity to Meta Business Cloud without intermediate brokers.

  • Step 1: Save Keys: Navigate to Settings > Integrations, expand "WhatsApp Settings", and enter your Page Token and Phone Number ID.
  • Step 2: Configure Webhook: Copy your dynamic Webhook URL (https://app.cleveradai.in/api/whatsapp/webhook?tenantId=YOUR_TENANT_ID) and paste it into Meta's App Settings. Set the Verification Token to gatidesk_verify_token.
  • Step 3: Subscribe to Fields: In Meta portal, check the messages change field. Real-time customer texts will now automatically spawn CRM chats.

2. Gmail OAuth Inbox Sync

Bi-directional Google OAuth synchronization using secure REST tokens.

  • Step 1: Authenticate Workspace: Under Integrations, click the primary "Connect Gmail" button to launch Google's secure OAuth flow.
  • Step 2: Approve Permissions: Log in with your corporate support account, verify scopes (Gmail read & send), and complete auth callback redirection.
  • Step 3: Pub/Sub Webhooks: Register watch webhook callbacks targeted on https://app.cleveradai.in/api/gmail/webhook?tenantId=YOUR_TENANT_ID. Incoming buyer mails will trigger real-time inbox threads.

3. Meta Platforms (Facebook & Instagram DMs)

Graph API ingestion routing messages from Messenger pages and Instagram Business profiles.

  • Step 1: Page Token: Enter your Facebook Page Token inside the integrations settings section.
  • Step 2: Configure Webhooks: Point Meta's Page Subscriptions callback to https://app.cleveradai.in/api/facebook/webhook?tenantId=YOUR_TENANT_ID. Set Verify Token to gatidesk_fb_verify_token.
  • Step 3: Test flow: Send a direct message to your Facebook Page; the AI receptionist will auto-score the profile and return responses.

4. Twilio Voice & Carrier SIP Factory

Provider-agnostic telephony routing supporting Twilio, Exotel, Plivo, Telnyx, Vonage, or direct trunk SIP trunks.

  • Step 1: Telephony Keys: Input your carrier credentials (e.g. Twilio SID & Token, or SIP SIP host details) and select your active telephony provider.
  • Step 2: Inbound Webhook: Copy the voice routing URL (https://app.cleveradai.in/api/voice/inbound?tenantId=YOUR_TENANT_ID) and configure it as the HTTP POST webhook for incoming calls in your carrier console.
  • Step 3: Audio Streams: Establish connection targets on wss://app.cleveradai.in/api/voice/stream to enable voice agents to handle real-time audio streams.
